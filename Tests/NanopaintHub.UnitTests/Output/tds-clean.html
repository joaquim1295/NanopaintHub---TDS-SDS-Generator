Piezoresistive Ink

NP-PI-1000 High Sensitivity Piezoresistive Ink · NP-PI-1000

High-performance carbon-based piezoresistive ink optimized for face-to-face printing on flexible substrates. Exhibits strong linear piezoresistive response with minimal hysteresis, suitable for wearable sensors and industrial force sensing.
